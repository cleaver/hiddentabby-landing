02 Your tabs stay yours

Private by design.

HiddenTabby stores workspace information in your current browser profile. It does not sync or upload that data to an external service.

Read the full privacy policy ↗
⌂

Stored locally

Workspace names, tab titles, and URLs stay in browser storage on this profile.

◌

No page contents

HiddenTabby does not read page contents, form entries, or cookies. Pinned and incognito tabs are excluded.

↻

Restore with care

Saved tabs can be reopened when you switch groups. Unsaved page state may not return.
